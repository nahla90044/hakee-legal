Help & Support

Contact us: nahlah@nahlah.io — we usually reply within two business days. Please mention your phone model, and the file name and page number if the problem is with reading.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Hakee works without sign-up. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID, and you can restore it on any device from “Restore purchases” in Settings.

How are pages counted?

Each page you hear for the first time counts once. Replaying any page you've already heard is always free, even offline. Your balance renews at the start of each month.

A page wasn't read, or was read partly

Scanned pages are read as images, so image quality affects accuracy. Empty pages or pages with only pictures are skipped automatically without being counted.

Why doesn't it read the page number or the header on every page?

Hakee skips things that repeat on every page (page numbers, headers, publisher marks) so you only hear the book itself. A chapter title is read once, at its start.

How do I change the voice or the language?

Tap the voice name in the player, or go to Settings. All 16 voices are available on every plan; dramatized reading (a different voice for each character) comes with Hakee Plus. You can switch the app between English and Arabic in Settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hakee. Cancelling stops renewal, and your subscription keeps working until the end of the paid period.

Deleting my files and data

Long-press any file in your library and choose “Delete”: it's removed from your phone and from our servers immediately. To delete all your other data from our servers, email us and we'll do it within 30 days.
